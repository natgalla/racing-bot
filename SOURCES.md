# Sources

This bot draws from the following sources:

- **Car specs and performance data** — [GT7 Car Database (gtdb.io)](https://gtdb.io), cached locally
- **Race schedule and spec** — League Discord pins and channel posts
- **Handicap adjustments** — League-internal Discord posts
- **Tuning recommendations** — Built-in tuning knowledge base
- **GT7 general info** — gran-turismo.com, gtplanet.net, gt7.fandom.com
- **Forza Motorsport info** — forzamotorsport.net, forza.fandom.com
- **Web search** — DuckDuckGo (last resort, cited inline when used)

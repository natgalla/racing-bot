Privacy Policy

Gateaubot — Last updated: September 2026

Gateaubot is a Discord bot built for a private sim racing server. It answers questions about race schedules, car specs, and tuning. This page explains what data the bot touches, what it sends off-server, and what it does not do.

What the bot reads

To do its job, Gateaubot reads the following from Discord:

  • Message content in channels it has access to
  • Pinned messages in those channels
  • Discord scheduled events on the server
  • Basic channel metadata (channel name, message timestamps)
  • The Discord username of the user whose message triggered a response

The bot scans messages passively to decide whether a message contains a racing-related question worth responding to. Most messages are evaluated and then discarded immediately.

What gets sent to HuggingFace

When Gateaubot decides to respond, it sends a short window of recent channel messages to HuggingFace's Inference API. HuggingFace hosts the language model (Qwen 72B) that generates the bot's replies.

Before any messages leave your server:

  • The username of the user who asked the question is included in the request. This is used to match their name against standings tables when answering handicap questions.
  • Usernames of other members appearing in recent channel messages are replaced with anonymous labels (User1, User2, and so on) before being transmitted.
  • Message content is sent only to produce a reply. It is not used for training.

HuggingFace's handling of Inference API requests is governed by their own privacy policy. Gateaubot uses the API in inference-only mode and does not opt in to any data-sharing or training programs.

What is not stored

Gateaubot does not maintain a database or any persistent storage of user data. Specifically:

  • No messages are saved to disk or a database.
  • No usernames or user IDs are recorded.
  • No logs of conversations are kept.
  • Nothing is retained after the bot finishes generating a reply.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell any data.
  • We do not share data with any third party other than HuggingFace for inference.
  • We do not use cookies or any form of tracking.
  • We do not require account registration of any kind.
  • We do not collect data from users outside the Discord server the bot is installed in.

Who operates this bot

Gateaubot is operated by a private individual for a private Discord server. It is not a commercial product or service.
